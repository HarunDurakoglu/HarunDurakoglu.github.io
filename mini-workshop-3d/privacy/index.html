Mini Workshop 3D

Calm 3D building game for children

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the current version of Mini Workshop 3D. The app is made for children, primarily ages 6–8, and is designed to work without collecting or transmitting personal information.

Information we collect

Mini Workshop 3D does not collect data. The app does not collect personal information, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, location, contacts, photos, audio, or any other user data.

Accounts and online services

The app has no account or login, backend, cloud service, advertising, analytics, tracking, artificial intelligence feature, in-app purchase, or user-generated content. It does not send gameplay information to the developer or to third parties.

Local app data

Level progress and the sound preference are stored only on the device. This local information is used to continue the game and remember the selected setting. It is not transmitted off the device.

Children’s privacy

Mini Workshop 3D is intended for children. The current version does not collect personal data, show advertising, enable communication between users, or link to social networks.

Regional availability

The app provides the same functionality in every region where it is available.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes the app’s features or privacy practices, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Questions

For privacy questions or assistance, visit the Mini Workshop 3D support page.
