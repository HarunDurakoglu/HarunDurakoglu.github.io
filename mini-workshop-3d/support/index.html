Mini Workshop 3D

Calm 3D building game for children

Help

Support

Parents and guardians can contact the developer for help with Mini Workshop 3D.

About the game

Mini Workshop 3D is an offline building game for children. Players drag colorful toy parts to matching numbered and colored slots across 50 levels.

Progress and sound

Level progress and the sound preference are saved locally on the device. If the app is deleted, this local progress may be removed by the operating system.
