About the app

Ehliyet Sınavı 2026 is an offline-first mobile study app featuring curriculum-aligned practice questions across First Aid, Traffic & Environment, Vehicle Technology, and Traffic Etiquette.

Restoring purchases

If you purchased the Lifetime Pro upgrade and changed devices or reinstalled the application, open the app, tap the Pro button, and select Satın Alımları Geri Yükle (Restore Purchases). Your purchase will automatically be verified against your Google Play account.

Official disclaimer

This application is an independent educational tool. It is not an official application of or endorsed by the Ministry of National Education (MEB). It does not guarantee passing the exam.
