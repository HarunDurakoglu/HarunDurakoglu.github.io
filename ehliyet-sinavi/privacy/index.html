Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices of Ehliyet Sınavı 2026 (package: com.harundurakoglu.ehliyetsinavi). The app is designed offline-first to help driver candidates practice for electronic exams without collecting personal identification.

Anonymous authentication and data collection

  • Anonymous sign-in: The app creates a random anonymous session with Firebase Authentication. No name, email, phone number, or passwords are ever requested.
  • Progress & favorites: Completed mock exams, scores, answered questions, and favorites are stored locally on your device in SQLite, and synced to Cloud Firestore under your private anonymous UID.
  • In-app purchase: Lifetime Pro upgrade (ehliyet_pro_lifetime) is verified via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. Payment and card details are processed by Google Play and never touch our servers.

Zero tracking & zero advertisements

  • No advertising SDKs or ad profiling.
  • No analytics or user tracking libraries.
  • No device permissions requested (no GPS, no contacts, no microphone, no camera).
  • Data is never shared with or sold to third parties.

Data deletion and reset

You can completely clear your data at any time. Opening the app and selecting Settings > Reset Progress & Data will immediately delete all local SQLite tables, remove Cloud Firestore progress records, clear the RevenueCat user association, and delete the Firebase anonymous user account.

Türkçe Gizlilik Politikası

Ehliyet Sınavı 2026 uygulaması sürücü adaylarının gizliliğine en üst düzeyde önem verir.

  • Kişisel Bilgi Toplanmaz: Uygulamayı kullanmak için ad, soyad, e-posta veya telefon gerekmez. Arka planda anonim bir kimlik (UID) kullanılır.
  • İlerleme Verileri: Çözdüğünüz sorular, puanlar ve favoriler cihazınızdaki SQLite veritabanında ve buluttaki anonim kaydınızda tutulur.

  • Tek Dokunuşla Silme: Ayarlar > Verileri Sıfırla seçeneği ile hem cihazdaki hem de buluttaki tüm verileriniz anında kalıcı olarak silinir.
