About Kalori Takip

Kalori Takip is a daily nutrition and meal tracking mobile app designed to help you stay mindful of your calorie intake, macronutrient distribution, and health goals.

Subscriptions and in-app purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled directly through Google Play or Apple App Store. You can review, cancel, or manage your active subscription at any time through your Google Play Store account settings under Subscriptions.

Account and data deletion

You have full control over your data. To permanently delete your account and all meal records, open the app, navigate to Settings > Delete Account, and confirm. All records in our database will be permanently removed.
