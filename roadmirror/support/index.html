Support, compatibility, and feedback information for RoadMirror.

About RoadMirror

RoadMirror allows users to mirror their phone screen to a compatible Android Auto car display after granting Android MediaProjection screen capture consent. You control apps directly from your phone.

Vehicle compatibility and safety

Vehicle testing has been confirmed on Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra with a 2024 Nissan Qashqai. Other vehicle and phone combinations are currently undergoing testing. Do not use video mirroring while driving; use only when safely parked or on passenger screens in full accordance with local traffic regulations.

Account and data deletion

If you created an optional RoadMirror account, you can delete it and its profile anytime directly in the app or by visiting the account deletion page.
